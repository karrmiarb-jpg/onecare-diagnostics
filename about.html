About OneCare

Accurate care, close by

OneCare Diagnostics is a DOH-licensed neighborhood lab in Dumanjug, serving Dumanjuganons and people from nearby towns since 2024.

Why we started OneCare

For many families here, a lab test used to mean a long line or a trip to the next town or the city. Some people put off a check-up until they already felt sick.

OneCare was started to bring accurate lab testing close to home, with a real welcome. A farmer and a business owner get the same smile, the same time and the same respect.

Our rule is simple: be right first, be kind always. When a test needs a better kit to be accurate, we use the better kit.

Led by a registered medical technologist

OneCare is owned and run by Karr Mia R. Boquecosa, RMT. She sets the standard for every result that leaves our lab, and makes sure every person at the door is treated the same way.

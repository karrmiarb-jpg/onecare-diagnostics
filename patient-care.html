Patient Care

Extra care for those who need it most

Everyone gets the same warm welcome at OneCare. Our senior citizens and cancer patients also get a few things that make the visit easier.

Senior Citizens' Corner

For our lolos and lolas

Priority lane

Senior citizens are served first. Just let us know when you arrive.

Assistance & seating

Our staff will help you in and out, and you'll have a seat while you wait.

20% senior discount

Bring your Senior Citizen ID. PWDs get the same 20% discount with their PWD ID.

Helpful tips

Bring a list of your medicines, wear sleeves that roll up easily, and come with a family member if you like.

Cancer Patients' Corner

Steady support through regular tests

Priority lane

If you're going through cancer treatment, you're served first. You shouldn't have to wait long when you're tired.

Assistance & seating

Our staff will help you, and you'll have a seat while you wait.

Regular CBC monitoring

Many patients on treatment need a CBC before each session. Bring your oncologist's request each time, and we'll take care of the rest.

Talk to us

If something about the visit is hard for you, tell us. We'll do our best to make it easier.

Coming with a senior or a loved one in treatment?

Call ahead and we'll be ready for you.

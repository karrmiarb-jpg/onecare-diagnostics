FAQ

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are welcome Mon–Sat, 7:30AM–5:00PM. Booking ahead just helps us get ready for you.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Only for some tests, like blood sugar (FBS) and cholesterol. Our Preparation page lists what each test needs.

How much will my tests cost?

Tick your tests on our Prices page to see an estimated total, with your discount already applied. The final price is confirmed at the lab.

Do you give senior and PWD discounts?

Yes. Senior citizens and PWDs get 25% off. Please bring your Senior Citizen or PWD ID.

Can you collect samples at home?

Yes, for those who can't come in. Call us to check if your area is covered.

Are screening tests like HIV private?

Yes. Screening tests are private and confidential. We explain them and ask for your consent first.
